CardScout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the CardScout mobile app for Android and iOS (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by Mikhail Kuznetsov ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the App's handling of personal data and can be contacted as described in section 12. The App lets you browse trading card catalogues (One Piece, Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering and others) and view public market prices and seller offers.

CardScout is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Cardmarket (Sammelkartenmarkt GmbH & Co. KG), Google, Scryfall, TCGdex, or any card game publisher.

1. Summary

  • The App has no user accounts, sign-in, or registration.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details.
  • We do not run our own servers and do not sell personal data. We can see crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics, which include installation and session identifiers but not your name or contact details.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • To show catalogues, prices and exchange rates, the App connects to third-party services (Cardmarket, Scryfall, TCGdex and currency rate services). These services receive standard technical data such as your IP address.

2. Information the App stores on your device

To work faster and use less data, the App keeps a local cache on your device of the public card catalogue, daily price lists and image folder lookups it downloads. This cache contains only public card data, not information about you. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Your search text is used only on your device and in requests to the card data services listed below to find matching cards. We do not keep a search history.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by banner ads and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (and Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). When ads are loaded, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), where permitted;
  • IP address, from which an approximate (coarse) location may be derived;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and App version;
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs of the SDK.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. It may also show personalised ads, with your consent where required. Google may link this data to your device or advertising identifiers. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the App shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are served. You can accept or refuse personalised advertising. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data for purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregated reporting.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising).

Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing ads. Time without ads earned from rewarded ads is stored only on your device, and is lost if you uninstall the App. The Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details or store account. The App only asks the store whether the purchase is on your account, and remembers the answer on your device. After the purchase, the App no longer starts the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

4. Third-party services the App connects to

The App downloads public card data and exchange rates directly from the services below. As with any internet request, these services receive your IP address, the requested address (which may include your search text), and standard technical information such as your device's user agent. Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy.

ServiceWhat the App loadsPrivacy policy
Cardmarket Product catalogue and price guide files, card images, and product pages with seller offers. cardmarket.com privacy
Scryfall Magic: The Gathering card search results. scryfall.com/docs/privacy
TCGdex Pokémon card data. tcgdex.dev
Currency rate services (jsDelivr, currency-api on Cloudflare Pages, Frankfurter) Daily exchange rates. jsDelivr, Cloudflare, Frankfurter
Google AdMob Banner ads, rewarded ads and the consent form. policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Crash reports: the stack trace, device model, operating system version, App version, and installation and session identifiers. They are used only to find and fix crashes. firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Seller offers and web content

To show current seller offers, the App opens Cardmarket's public product pages in a hidden in-app web view (the system WebView on Android, WKWebView on iOS). Cardmarket and its security provider (such as Cloudflare) may set cookies in that web view and may run a browser check to protect their site. These cookies are stored only in the App's web view on your device. We do not read them for any other purpose and do not send them to anyone. You do not sign in to Cardmarket inside the App.

If you tap a link to open a card on Cardmarket, it opens in your browser, and Cardmarket's own privacy policy applies there.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not use our own analytics or tracking tools. Crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics are used only to fix crashes.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files outside the App, or precise location. On Android, the App requests internet access and the permissions its ads and in-app purchase components need, such as the advertising ID, network state and Google Play billing.
  • We do not share data with data brokers.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies: personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention, crash reporting and the technical requests needed to load card data and exchange rates are based on legitimate interests in providing, funding and keeping stable a free app.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not run our own servers. Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports and their identifiers for 90 days before starting to remove them. You can remove all data the App stores on your device by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Other data collected by Google or the other services listed above is retained and deleted according to their own policies; you can manage Google ad data at myadcenter.google.com.

8. Security

All network requests made by the App use encrypted HTTPS connections.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. The only data linked to you that we can see is the crash report data described above, and we have no name or contact details to match it to you. Requests about advertising data should be directed to Google; we are happy to help you with any question.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App gains new features. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
