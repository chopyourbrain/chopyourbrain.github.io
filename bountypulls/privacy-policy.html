Bounty Pulls Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Bounty Pulls ("the app", "we") is a free, unofficial fan-made pack-opening simulator for the ONE PIECE Card Game, available for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have.

The short version: the app has no accounts and we run no servers of our own. Your collection stays on your device. The app shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information to serve and measure those ads, subject to your consent where the law requires it.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • your collection: the binders, opened boxes, pulled cards and grades;
  • your coins, free-pack timer, daily ad count and streak;
  • your settings, such as "Reduce motion".

We never receive this information. It is not linked to your name or any account. It may be included in your device's own backup (Android Backup or iCloud backup) if you have turned backups on; those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own terms. Uninstalling the app deletes this data from the device.

2. Card data and images

To show sets and cards, the app downloads card lists and card images from optcgapi.com and pack images from images.scrydex.com. Like any web request, these requests reveal your IP address and basic technical details (such as the app's network library and the time of the request) to those sites. The app sends them nothing else about you. These services are run by third parties, not by us, and their own privacy practices apply.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a rewarded video only when you tap "Watch an ad" to earn free in-app coins. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To serve, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking, see section 5);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads (for example whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped).

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Coins are virtual, free, and have no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for real money, cards or prizes.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can choose whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices (shown wherever the consent message applies). Where state privacy laws in the United States give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, Google's consent message may offer that choice too.

5. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, after the consent step, the app asks for your permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalised ads. If you decline, the app works exactly the same and ads are not tailored using the IDFA. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

6. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that resulted in information being collected, contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is under your control: uninstalling the app removes it. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use the choices above or contact Google through https://policies.google.com/privacy. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
